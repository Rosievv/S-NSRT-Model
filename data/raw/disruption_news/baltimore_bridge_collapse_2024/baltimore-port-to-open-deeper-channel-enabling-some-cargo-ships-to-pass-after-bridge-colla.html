Baltimore port to open deeper channel, enabling some cargo ships to pass after bridge collapse

  • Baltimore officials will open a deeper channel for ships to access the city's port, post Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse, slightly ahead of schedule.
  • The new channel is 35 feet deep, exceeding temporary channels set up before, aiding in the cleanup effort.
  • A Maryland pilot and two tugboats are required for ship passage, with the main port channel set to reopen next month.
