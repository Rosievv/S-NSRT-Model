Yantian Port restarts receiving containers

From: Shenzhen Daily | Updated:2021-06-02

打印

分享到：

Yantian Port resumed receiving export containers Monday, and will only accept those which will be picked up by container ships that are estimated to dock at the port within three days. 

The port has backlogged more than 20,000 containers but is still expecting to receive about 5,000 a day until Sunday. The port stopped accepting containers May 25, as a result of a severe delay for carriers.
