MEDIA RELEASE 
 
MITI LEADS CIMS 3.0 COORDINATION TO EXPEDITE APPROVALS AND 
FACILITATE SMOOTH RUNNING OF THE ECONOMY DURING  
MCO 3.0 (1 -14 JUNE 2021) 
_________________________________________________________________  
 
Yesterday, the Sidang Khas Majlis Keselamatan Negara, decided that approval 
of companies under the economic sectors allowed to operate during the 
Movement Control Order (MCO) (commencing 1st June to 14th June 2021) is to 
be obtained via COVID-19 Intelligent Management System ( CIMS) 3.0 
administered by the Ministry of International Trade and Industry (MITI). 
Pursuant to this, MITI immediately held discussions with the relevant Ministries 
in order to implement the latest decision. As of toda y, nine (9) ministries have 
provided their respective approved list of economic sectors for MITI to issue the 
approval letters to companies registered with CIMS 3.0.  
MITI has set  up a special operation center  to ensure that th e CIMS 3.0 runs 
smoothly and effectively. The Senior Minister and Minister of MITI , Deputy 
Minister of MITI  and top MITI officials and agencies are on si te to ensure 
effective coordination within ministries , for the smooth processing of  
applications that will facilitate economic activities in accordance with the  new 
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) under the MCO.  
As at 12 noon today, of the 517,144 companies  with a total workforce of 9.67 
million registered under CIMS  3.0, 95,142 companies have obtained their 
approval to operate during the MCO with strict compliance to the prescribed 
new SOPs. As of now, b ased on the NSC imposition of workforce capacity, it 
is estimated that 1.2 million workforce of the approved companies are allowed 

2 
 
to work at premises . This effectively amounts to a reduction of 2 million 
workforce of the 3.2 million total workforce for the approved companies. 
For companies under the approved sector s which did not register with CIMS  
3.0 prior to this latest MC O, they are advised to do  so should they wish to 
operate during this MCO.  MITI has started processing such applications from 
companies under the approved sectors from 1.00 pm, 31st May 2021. 
All approval letters issued by MITI via CIMS 3.0 should bear the QR code. This 
feature is necessary to allow the authorities and the enforcement agencies to 
verify the authenticity of the approval letters.
